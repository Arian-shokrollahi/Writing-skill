# My childhood and how it shaped my personality
---
### **نسخه اصلاح‌شده و طبیعی متن تو**

My full name is Arian Shokrollahi. I'm 20 years old, and as far as I can remember, I had a happy childhood because my parents weren't strict. They let me play with other children in the street, and they always gave me good advice and took care of me. They also taught me useful things about how to communicate with other people. Their behavior helped shape my personality.

### **سه نکته خیلی مهم**

**1. Advice و Advise**

- `Advice` اسم است: توصیه
    
- `Advise` فعل است: توصیه کردن
    

مثال:

- My parents gave me good advice.
    
- والدینم توصیه‌های خوبی به من کردند.
    

**2. Let me play**

بعد از `let` فعل بدون `to` می‌آید:

- They let me play.
    
    درست
    
- They let me to play.
    
    غلط
    

**3. Shape my personality**

عبارت `shape my personality` یعنی شخصیت من را شکل دادن.

مثلاً:

My childhood experiences shaped my personality.

تجربیات دوران کودکی من شخصیتم را شکل دادند.
