# My Best Friend

My best friend is Parsa, an old school friend of mine. Although I'm one year older than him, we went to the same school and we were very close. We still are.

We first got to know each other after class. We saw each other in the schoolyard, sat next to each other, and ate our meals together. After eating, we started talking and getting to know each other better. We talked about different things, laughed a lot, and slowly became good friends. At first, we were just classmates, but after some time, we became best friends. We have many good memories from our school days, and I really enjoyed spending time with him.

We've spent a lot of time together, and we're really close friends. Our families know each other, too. We have always had a good friendship, and distance hasn't changed that.

Now, I'm still living in Iran, but he has moved to Canada. He's currently a university student, and he's studying computer science there. Even though we're far away from each other, we still keep in touch. We call each other every day and talk about our lives, our experiences, and what's happening around us.

I miss the days when we could see each other at school and spend time together, but I'm happy that we're still such good friends. I hope we can see each other again soon.

---
**چند عبارت قشنگ که به متن اضافه کردیم:**

- **Slowly became good friends** = کم‌کم دوستان خوبی شدیم.
    
- **We have many good memories** = خاطرات خوب زیادی داریم.
    
- **Distance hasn't changed that** = فاصله این موضوع را تغییر نداده است.
    
- **We still keep in touch** = هنوز با هم در ارتباط هستیم.
    
- **I miss the days when...** = دلم برای روزهایی که... تنگ شده.
    
- **I hope we can see each other again soon.** = امیدوارم به‌زودی دوباره همدیگر را ببینیم.
